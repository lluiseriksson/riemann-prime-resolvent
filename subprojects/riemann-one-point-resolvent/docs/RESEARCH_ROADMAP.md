# Research roadmap

## Milestone A — formal analytic core

- Define project-normalized \(\xi\) and \(\Xi\) from Mathlib’s completed zeta API.
- Prove the equivalence with Mathlib’s `RiemannHypothesis` proposition.
- Formalize the slit plane and the map \(z\mapsto-z^2\) from the upper half-plane.
- Formalize logarithmic-derivative poles at zeros of an analytic function.
- Prove the slit-plane extension criterion.

## Milestone B — infinite moment bridge

- Formalize the Hausdorff moment theorem in the exact finite-measure form needed here.
- Prove the one-point derivative-jet equivalence.
- Derive scalar finite-difference, Hankel, and localizing formulations.

## Milestone C — arithmetic target

- Prove the completed-zeta logarithmic derivative formula.
- Formalize the von Mangoldt Dirichlet series on \(\Re s>1\).
- Prove the explicit tail bound uniformly on compact real intervals.

## Milestone D — certified finite approximants

- Define exact Hermitian matrix certificates.
- Check residuals, spectral gaps, and trace-resolvent interval bounds.
- Produce machine-readable certificate schemas.
- Connect finite certificates to a mathematically defined infinite model.

## Milestone E — substantive analytic contribution

Prove at least one nontrivial estimate for a concrete prime-built operator family:

- a uniform one-point trace-resolvent bound;
- a Galerkin error estimate;
- a ground-state alignment estimate;
- a model-normalization estimate;
- or full prime–resolvent convergence on an interval.

A claim of RH requires full convergence plus independent expert review and a circularity audit.
