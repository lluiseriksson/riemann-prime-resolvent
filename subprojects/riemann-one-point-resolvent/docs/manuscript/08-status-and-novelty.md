# Status, novelty and publication boundary

## Classical ingredients

Stieltjes transforms, Hausdorff moments, logarithmic-derivative poles, Montel compactness and moment criteria related to RH have prior literature. The repository does not claim those ingredients as new.

## Potential contribution

A useful contribution may lie in the precise integrated package:

- the squared-resolvent target;
- a one-point derivative jet;
- explicit slit-plane reconstruction;
- finite signed-difference and PSD certificates;
- an integer-cutoff prime tail;
- a two-repository criterion/construction contract;
- formal verification with explicit status labels.

Novelty of the exact formulation requires theorem-level literature review.

## Open decisive step

The companion repository must prove a non-circular convergence estimate for a concrete prime-built self-adjoint family. Until then, this is a reduction and formalization programme, not an RH proof.
