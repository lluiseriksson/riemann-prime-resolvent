# Certified localized positivity at support 0.54

## Theorem

Let $A_a$ be Suzuki's Friedrichs operator for the localized Weil quadratic
form, with the normalization used throughout this directory. Then

\[
 A_{0.54}\succeq 7.13337959131472\cdot10^{-9}I>0.
\]

This statement is unconditional. It proves positivity at the single support
radius $a=0.54$; it does not prove positivity on an interval and does not prove
the Riemann hypothesis.

## Second Green discharge of the common-floor gate

For the touching blocks, polynomial division writes the second Green image as
a polynomial plus $R(U)\log U$. The Legendre coefficients of the singular
part are exact rational functions of the degrees, so their signed moments can
be retained. Arb Taylor jets and Wang's derivative bound control the analytic
remainder. At $a=0.54$ this gives

| component | certified upper bound |
|---|---:|
| self block above degree 128 | 13.09239946328302 |
| adjacent edge-to-centre block | 13.46839524666344 |
| adjacent centre-to-edge block | 6.69859812529535 |
| separated edge block | 1.301283532240199 |
| global regularized map | 33.44087021675598 |

The resulting degree-16/128 Schur certificate at 1024 bits gives

| parity | shift | negative | positive | unresolved | first positive lower |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| even | 0.0011 | 1 | 23 | 0 | 0.0010809213652708614 |
| odd | 0.1 | 1 | 23 | 0 | 0.026232727283724652 |

The common complement floor is 1.2850480151860335. Hence the second spectral
points are at least 0.0011 and 0.1 in the even and odd sectors.

## Stable certified prime action

The prime-two action is evaluated without quadrature. Two interval-dependency
failures must be avoided at this scale:

1. truncated powers are generated by integration by parts rather than repeated
   application of $X-c$;
2. the Legendre recurrence is restarted every 4096 degrees from a direct Arb
   evaluation of $P_n(c)$.

Every restart is an enclosing Arb computation. Non-finite midpoint or radius
exports are rejected. A checkpoint runner stores the completed prime action
with its parameter tuple and SHA-256 hash of the trial vector before assembling
the final certificate.

The top-level from-scratch reproducer is `support_054_certificate.py`.

The relative-form argument in `support-054-interval.md` turns this point into
a nonzero certified neighbourhood. Its radius is extremely small because
zero-extended translations are only logarithmically continuous on the form
domain; no numerically visible interval is claimed.
