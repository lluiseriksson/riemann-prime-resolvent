# Paper plan

## Safe first paper

**Working title:** *A Formal Prime–Resolvent Research Programme for the Riemann
Xi Function*

Target scope:

1. exact Xi/Mathlib bridge;
2. slit-plane extension criterion;
3. Stieltjes compactness criterion;
4. explicit prime-tail bound;
5. finite certificate architecture;
6. Lean formalization and reproducibility;
7. concrete spectral construction stated conditionally.

This is publishable as a formalization/reduction paper if the abstract chain is
complete and the literature review confirms the formulation adds value.

## Stronger second paper

Add a new theorem controlling at least one concrete term:

- Galerkin error;
- residual/gap alignment;
- trace-resolvent approximation;
- source-audited model/prolate estimate;
- certified growing-parameter regime.

## Forbidden title before the final gate

Do not use “Proof of the Riemann Hypothesis” or wording that implies the open
spectral convergence has been solved.
