# Literature positioning

## Closest known themes

1. **Hausdorff moment criteria for RH.** Ruiming Zhang, *An Application of Hausdorff Moment Problem* (arXiv:2303.09396), gives families of moment-based necessary and sufficient conditions. This makes a careful novelty comparison mandatory.
2. **Prime-built self-adjoint approximants.** Connes, Consani, and Moscovici, *Zeta Spectral Triples* (arXiv:2511.22755), construct spectral approximants from truncated Euler products and identify rigorous convergence as the decisive open step.
3. **Real-zero mechanisms from self-adjoint operators.** Connes and van Suijlekom, *Quadratic Forms, Real Zeros and Echoes of the Spectral Action* (arXiv:2511.23257), prove real-zero conclusions under specific self-adjoint spectral hypotheses.
4. **Classical background.** Riemann’s explicit-formula programme, Hadamard products, Stieltjes transforms, Hausdorff moments, Montel normal families, and Hurwitz-type zero arguments.

## Plausible contribution of this project

The potentially distinct package is the simultaneous use of:

- the squared-resolvent target \(\mathcal S_\Xi\);
- a derivative jet at **one fixed point**;
- resolvent compactification to a Hausdorff measure on \([0,1]\);
- a slit-plane extension proof excluding non-real zeros;
- finite PSD certificates and formal verification;
- an explicit prime-side truncation target.

No novelty claim should be made until a line-by-line comparison with Zhang and related derivative/moment criteria has been reviewed by specialists.
