# [RF-3] Construct and certify the concrete spectral model

<!-- frontier-id: RF-3 -->

## Goal

Define a concrete operator or Galerkin family and prove the structural and
approximation properties required by the resolvent programme.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Hilbert space, domain, operator and approximants are precisely defined.
- [ ] Required self-adjointness and compactness/resolvent properties are proved.
- [ ] Finite-to-infinite approximation bounds are certified.
- [ ] Constants are exposed through the interface contract.
- [ ] A no-circularity audit lists every analytic input.

## Non-goals

Naming an operator without proofs; treating numerical eigenvalue agreement as
convergence.
