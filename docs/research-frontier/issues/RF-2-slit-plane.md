# [RF-2] Prove the slit-plane Stieltjes criterion for Xi zeros

<!-- frontier-id: RF-2 -->

## Goal

Prove `XiStieltjesExtensionTarget → XiOnlyRealZeros` with all analytic
hypotheses made explicit.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Branch, domain, regularity and nonvanishing hypotheses are precise.
- [ ] Multiplicities and logarithmic-derivative singularities are handled.
- [ ] The implication is proved in Lean without project axioms.
- [ ] The relationship with Mathlib's RH statement is documented accurately.
- [ ] Existence of the extension is not silently assumed.

## Non-goals

Claiming the extension exists; replacing proof by finite zero verification.
