# Architectural decisions

1. **Standalone repository.** The topic is separate from the Yang–Mills project.
2. **No research axioms.** Open results remain hypotheses or named Props.
3. **Abstract analytic chain first.** Concrete operators come after Xi/slit/prime infrastructure.
4. **Squared resolvent observable.** It is positive and less normalization-sensitive than determinants.
5. **Certificate-driven numerics.** Floating-point output is never trusted directly.
6. **Pinned environment.** Upgrades happen only on dedicated branches.
7. **No RH branding.** The project is a research programme until the final gate.
