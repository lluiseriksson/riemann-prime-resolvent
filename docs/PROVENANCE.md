# Provenance

This artifact consolidates and sharpens the user’s research notes on:

- the squared-resolvent/Stieltjes target for the Riemann Xi function;
- a slit-plane extension criterion;
- a prime-side logarithmic-derivative expansion;
- one-point Hausdorff moments and finite certificates;
- Lean 4 formalization and reproducibility gates.

The manuscript was rewritten as a self-contained paper. Conceptual material concerning Gödel spacetime, Barbour time, Yang–Mills, and broader philosophical analogies is intentionally kept outside the core proof dependency graph. Those themes may motivate future expository work, but they are not hypotheses of the Riemann criterion.
