# No claim of a proof of the Riemann hypothesis

This repository does not prove RH. It formalizes finite and scalar components of a research programme and records the remaining obligations explicitly.

A future RH-level claim would require, at minimum, a non-circular construction of the concrete operators, domain and self-adjointness proofs, trace-class control, correct multiplicities and normalizations, one-point compactness, interval convergence to the completed-zeta target, closure of the companion criterion in Lean, and independent expert review.

Numerical agreement, finite matrix certificates, a green build, or a proposition packaged as an assumption is not a substitute for those obligations.
