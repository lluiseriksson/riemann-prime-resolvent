# No-RH-claim notice

This repository is **not a proof of the Riemann hypothesis**.

It contains:

- an abstract Stieltjes/slit-plane strategy;
- elementary Lean-verified bookkeeping;
- candidate quantitative estimates requiring independent checking;
- a plan for integrating concrete spectral operators;
- a paper draft explicitly labelled as a research programme.

The following are not established here:

1. the full concrete operator construction in Lean;
2. uniform simplicity/parity of the lowest state;
3. the required spectral alignment rate;
4. trace-class convergence of the squared resolvents;
5. the equivalence between the proposed extension criterion and Mathlib's
   `RiemannHypothesis` in Lean;
6. the Riemann hypothesis itself.

Any commit, README, abstract, release, or paper that removes these qualifications
must first satisfy `PUBLICATION-GATE.md` and undergo expert review.
